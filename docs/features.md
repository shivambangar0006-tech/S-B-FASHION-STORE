# Feature Specification

## Product Management

Each product will eventually contain:

- Product name
- Price
- Sale price
- Images
- Category
- Description
- Sizes
- Colors
- Stock quantity
- Product status
- Created/updated dates

## Reviews

Customers can:

- Give 1–5 stars.
- Write a review.
- See existing reviews.
- Optionally upload a product photo.

Admins can moderate reviews.

## Suggestions

The contact area will include a suggestion form for customer feedback.

## Couples

The Couples section will contain coordinated products and complete looks.

## Build Your Look

Customers can choose:

- Model settings
- Top
- Bottom
- Shoes
- Accessories

A complete look can later be saved or added to the cart.

## AI Assistant

The assistant will help with:

- Product discovery
- Store questions
- Size guidance
- Order support

An API-based implementation will be added later with secrets kept on the server.
